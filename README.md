# Data-Professional-Survey
This dashboard presents a comprehensive overview of insights from a survey of data professionals, highlighting trends across salary, geography, skills, job roles, and workplace satisfaction. It shows the distribution of respondents by country, with the majority of survey takers located in the United States, followed by India, the United Kingdom, Canada, and other regions. A total of 630 respondents participated in the survey, with an average respondent age of approximately 30 years.

The dashboard also compares average salaries across job titles, indicating that Data Scientists and Data Engineers earn higher average salaries compared to other data-related roles. In terms of technical skills, Python emerges as the most popular programming language among respondents, significantly outperforming other languages such as R, C/C++, JavaScript, and Java.

Workplace experience and satisfaction are captured through self-reported measures. The difficulty of working in data roles is mostly rated as moderate to difficult, suggesting a technically demanding field. Despite these challenges, respondents report relatively positive outcomes, with an average happiness score of 4.27 for salary and 5.74 for work–life balance (on a scale of 0–10).

Overall, the dashboard provides a high-level snapshot of the global data professional landscape, combining career outcomes, skill preferences, and well-being indicators to support workforce analysis, career planning, and industry benchmarking.
